## Method 1: Drag & Drop Directly into Figma (Fastest — 2 Seconds)
1. Open [https://www.figma.com](https://www.figma.com) and create a new Design File.
2. Drag and drop **`ROADWATCH_All_20_Figma_Frames.svg`** (or select all 20 `.svg` files inside `figma_frames/Frame_01_*.svg` through `Frame_20_*.svg`) directly onto your Figma canvas.
3. Figma natively parses every screen into editable top-level Figma Frames (`1440 x 900` Desktop and `390 x 844` Mobile), complete with editable text layers, vector icons, PostGIS maps, tables, and named component groups.

---

## Method 2: 1-Click Copy & Paste (`Cmd+V` / `Ctrl+V`) from Interactive Showcase
1. Open **`wireframes/index.html`** in Google Chrome or Safari.
2. Click **"Copy All 20 Frames for Figma (⌘V)"** in the top navigation bar (or click **"Copy SVG for Figma (⌘V)"** on any individual frame card).
3. Switch to your open Figma file and press **`Cmd+V`** (Mac) or **`Ctrl+V`** (Windows).
